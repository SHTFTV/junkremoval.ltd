The practical tool

A clearer brief. A better conversation.

Write down the scope before asking for a quote. Create and download a practical brief for the providers you choose.

This tool runs in your browser. Entries are not submitted to us or sent to a removal provider. A download is a local file, not a booking.

What to include

  • Ownership and removal authority confirmed.
  • Items to keep clearly distinguished from removal items.
  • Access, carrying distances and timing constraints.
  • Separate destination checks for restricted materials.
  • How quote changes and final destination records will be handled.

Before anyone arrives

Confirm acceptance with the actual destination and ask the operator what the quote includes. Avoid moving heavy, unstable or unknown hazardous items simply to improve a photograph.
