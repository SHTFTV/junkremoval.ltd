What we are looking for

Practical expertise on reuse logistics, material routing, property cleanouts, equipment selection and documented operator workflows. Explain the audience, jurisdiction, real problem and evidence. Clearly distinguish firsthand experience, a hypothetical example and promotional claims.

Prepare a reviewable brief

Include a proposed headline, 150-word summary, relevant jurisdiction, primary source links, your professional role and any commercial interest. Do not include private customer addresses, account credentials, identifying case details or images without permission.

Submit a proposal using our public GitHub issue form. A GitHub account is required. Proposals are public; do not include private personal or customer information. Submission creates a repository issue for editorial review; it does not guarantee publication.
