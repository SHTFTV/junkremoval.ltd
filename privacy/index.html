Privacy

A small data footprint.

Understand what happens when you read a guide or use the clearout planner.

Planner entries

The clearout planner processes entries in the current browser page. It does not send your project to our server, a removal company or an email service. Downloading creates a file on your device. This version does not save entries in local storage; reloading the page clears them.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel. Hosting infrastructure may process request information, such as IP address and browser information, to serve and protect the site. The stylesheet requests Google Fonts, which involves a connection to that provider. No advertising tracker or analytics tag is intentionally installed in this launch build.

External links

External source and related-publication links take you to other services with their own practices. Do not include customer or sensitive personal information in any public contribution channel.

Changes

This notice describes the launch build. If a working contribution form, account or additional data service is added, its data handling must be documented before use.
