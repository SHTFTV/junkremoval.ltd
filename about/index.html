Our approach

Clear thinking before collection.

JunkRemoval.ltd is an independent editorial resource about clearouts, reuse, materials and the removal industry.

PurposeEditorial standardsGeographyContributions & corrections

Help people make a better next decision

Our work starts at the point where a possession becomes a question. Should it stay, go to another person, be assessed for reuse, enter an accepted recycling stream or be collected for disposal? Households, property managers and operators share that question, but have different responsibilities. We write distinct guides for those audiences.

We do not operate a removal business, maintain a provider directory, take bookings or promise service in a particular location. Links to related portfolio publications are editorial navigation, not claims that those sites provide a collection service.

Sources, images and claims

Guides link to primary authorities, public programmes and manufacturer documentation where relevant. Our original workflows, hypothetical examples and planning questions help interpret those sources. They are not claims that we inspected a particular job, interviewed an operator or measured a real customer's results.

Launch illustrations are original editorial artwork, labelled as illustrations. They do not depict verified collection companies, actual equipment configurations or completed customer jobs. We do not use fabricated testimonials or invented prices.

We distinguish collection, acceptance, reuse and final processing. A pickup receipt is not proof that an item was reused. An operator's marketing percentage is not automatically an independently verified recovery rate.

Local questions need local answers

Our launch coverage uses Canadian and other Commonwealth examples. Each named jurisdiction has its own authorities, material rules, programme boundaries and documentation requirements. A process that applies in England is not presented as a Canadian rule, and a municipal acceptance page is not treated as a national standard.

Check the current named authority, facility or programme before making a material-routing decision. We do not publish universal dump fees, imply universal licensing requirements or treat household and commercial streams as interchangeable.

Corrections and specialist contributions

We welcome precise corrections backed by primary sources. Our contribution page explains the working submission options and review criteria. Corrections should identify the exact guide, disputed statement, jurisdiction and replacement evidence.
